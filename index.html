Our story

Born from a love of café culture

Inspired by the warmth of a Parisian neighbourhood café, Ooh La La is a relaxed, stylish place to eat, drink and unwind in Enfield. Every visit should feel like a small escape: freshly brewed speciality coffee, beautifully presented food and the gentle buzz of conversation.

Our breakfast, brunch and lunch menus bring together Mediterranean flavours and quality ingredients, prepared with care, whether you're in for a morning coffee, a long brunch with friends or a celebration.

Mediterranean mornings
Breakfast platters with kaymak, honey, sujuk and Turkish tea, for one or to share.
Free-range eggs
Benedicts, shakshuka and full breakfasts, with sourdough and Halal options.
Afternoon cocktails
Cocktails and mocktails from midday, alongside wine, beer and soft drinks.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Do you take walk-ins?

Absolutely, walk-ins are welcome every day. If you’d like to guarantee a table, you can book online and get instant confirmation. For larger groups, give us a call, or have a look at private hire for celebrations.

Book a table →
What are your opening hours?

We're open Monday to Sunday, 8am – 5pm. Last food orders are taken at 4:30pm.

Is there parking nearby?

Yes. There is on-street parking along Hertford Road with a mix of free and pay-and-display bays. Enfield Retail Park (a short walk) also has a large free car park.

Can you cater for dietary requirements?

Yes, we have vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-aware options on the menu. Please let your server know of any allergies when you arrive and we'll guide you through the safest choices.

Do you have Halal options?

Yes, Halal options are available. Please ask your server and they will be happy to guide you to the Halal dishes on our menu.

Do you serve alcohol?

We serve a curated afternoon cocktail and mocktail menu from midday onwards, alongside wines, beers, and soft drinks.

Can I hire the venue for a private event?

Yes, we love hosting group brunches, birthday celebrations, and corporate events. Use our private hire enquiry form and we'll get back to you within 48 hours.
