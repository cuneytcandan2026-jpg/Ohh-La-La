Brunch, Speciality Coffee & Mediterranean Flavours in Enfield

Ooh La La Brasserie serves expertly crafted speciality coffee alongside fresh, beautifully presented breakfast, brunch and lunch dishes inspired by the Mediterranean. Set in a relaxed yet stylish space in Enfield, North London, it's the perfect place for morning coffee, leisurely catch-ups and special occasions.

Our Story

Born from a
Love of Café Culture

Inspired by the warmth and charm of a Parisian neighbourhood café, Ooh La La Brasserie is a relaxed and stylish place to eat, drink and unwind in the heart of Enfield. Every visit is designed to feel like a small escape, from the aroma of freshly brewed speciality coffee to beautifully presented food and the gentle buzz of conversation.

Our breakfast, brunch and lunch menus bring together Mediterranean-inspired flavours, quality ingredients and dishes prepared with care. Whether you are stopping by for your morning coffee, catching up with friends over brunch or celebrating a special occasion, you will always receive a warm welcome at Ooh La La.
